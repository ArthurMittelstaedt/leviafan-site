# Delete your data – Snake 3D

Last updated: October 23, 2026

**Snake 3D** is published on Google Play by **Arthur Mittelstaedt EI (Leviafan)**. We have no server and no account of our own: we hold no data about you. Your game data is on your device and in Google services linked to your account. Here is how to delete it.

## 1. Data on your device

Your progress (scores, unlocked content, quests, delivered purchases, settings).

- **Uninstall the Game** (without ticking "Keep app data" if Android offers it),
- or **clear its data**: Android Settings → Apps → Snake 3D → Storage → Clear data.

## 2. Your device's Google backup

If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android keeps a copy of your progress there.

- In Android's settings (Google → Backup), you can turn backup off and delete the device's backup.
- Google also deletes the backup of a device that has not been used for some time.

## 3. Your Google Play Games data

If you signed in to Google Play Games in the Game: your leaderboard scores, your cloud save and your activity in the Game.

- In the **Google Play Games** app: your profile picture → Settings → **Delete Play Games account & data** → **Delete individual game data** → Snake 3D. Only Snake 3D's data is deleted.
- You can also delete your whole Play Games account there, or manage your data from your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).

## What is deleted, what is kept

- **Deleted**: your progress, your scores and your cloud save, following the steps above. Deletion is permanent.
- **Kept by Google**: your in-app purchase history (Google Play keeps it for its legal and accounting obligations) and AdMob's advertising data, under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your permanent purchases (for example "No ads") stay linked to your Google account and come back if you reinstall the Game.
